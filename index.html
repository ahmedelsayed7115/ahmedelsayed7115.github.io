Consumer Credit Risk & Loan Default Intelligence

Multi-factor credit underwriting and default diagnostic across 601 consumer loans, isolating FICO score thresholds, Debt-to-Income (DTI) inflection boundaries, and loan purpose ticket-size exposure.

  • FICO Score Cliff: Subprime borrowers (520–599) default at 49.14% (57/116) — 4.2× higher than Very Good/Exceptional borrowers (750+ at 11.69%).
  • DTI Structural Inflection at 40%: Defaulters carry a 57.9% average DTI vs. 47.1% for performing loans; default triples from 11.89% (20–40% DTI) to 36.05% (60%+ DTI).
  • Ticket-Size & Tenure Amplification: Defaulted Education (+71%), Medical (+47%), and Debt Consolidation (+38%) loans carry significantly higher principal balances, while <2 yr tenure defaults at 34.52%.
